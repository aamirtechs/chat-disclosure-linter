Lint chat transcripts for required debt relief disclosures and prohibited claims, with line level evidence, before QA ever opens the conversation.

Disclaimer: This is a QA and compliance-support tool, not legal advice. A lint pass does not prove a conversation was compliant, and a flagged line may be acceptable in context. Review flagged lines with your QA and compliance leads.

Runs 100% in your browser. Your files never leave this device.

How it works

  • Paste a transcript or upload a .txt or .json file in LiveChat or Chatwoot style.
  • Required disclosures are searched only in agent messages. Customer words never satisfy a disclosure.
  • Prohibited patterns are flagged with the line number and the exact quoted line.
  • Mark a hit as acceptable in context with a reason when QA judges it fine. It stays listed but leaves the fail count.
  • Download the report CSV with required, prohibited, and feedback rows.
